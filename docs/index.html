How to Subscribe to Your Calendar

📱 iPhone / Apple Calendar / Mac

Click "Subscribe" on your card. iOS/macOS will open Calendar automatically with a prompt to subscribe.

📅 Google Calendar

Click "Copy URL". Go to Google Calendar > Other calendars (+) > From URL > Paste the link.

🔄 Auto-Updates

Once subscribed, your calendar automatically syncs chores, trash days, and house events whenever the schedule updates!
